Epoxy Flooring in Fleming Island, FL

Premium garage and commercial floor coatings for Fleming Island's planned communities

Fleming Island is one of Clay County's most sought-after addresses — master-planned communities, waterfront lots on Doctor's Lake, and homes built to impress inside and out. Yet even in Fleming Island's well-constructed homes, Florida's relentless humidity finds its way into concrete garage slabs. With humidity levels pushing 80% in summer months, moisture vapor migrates upward through concrete, setting the stage for peeling, staining, and surface degradation on unprotected floors.

Fleming Island homeowners invest in quality throughout their homes. The garage shouldn't be an afterthought. Our epoxy flooring systems turn that utilitarian slab into a seamless, showroom-quality surface that's as functional as it is beautiful — resisting oil, chemicals, and moisture while standing up to Florida's climate year after year.

We've coated hundreds of garages in Fleming Island's neighborhoods — Eagle Harbor, Fleming Island Plantation, and the custom waterfront estates along Doctor's Lake Drive. Whether you want a classic granite-flake floor for a clean, professional look or a flowing metallic epoxy that makes your garage the envy of the neighborhood, we deliver premium results with our 15-year warranty.

Our Installation Process

Every Fleming Island job follows our proven 5-step installation:

  1. Surface grinding — Diamond grinding equipment profiles the concrete for superior adhesion
  2. Moisture barrier primer — Penetrating primer blocks vapor transmission through the slab
  3. Base coat — High-build 100% solids epoxy creates a durable, uniform foundation
  4. Decorative topcoat — Your choice of flake, metallic, or solid color finish
  5. 24-hour cure — Walk on it the next day; drive on it after 72 hours
