Fernandina Beach is the charming heart of Amelia Island, a barrier island community of approximately 13,500 residents in Nassau County at the northeastern tip of Florida. Known for its beautifully preserved Victorian-era downtown along Centre Street, its shrimping heritage celebrated each year at the Isle of Eight Flags Shrimp Festival, and its miles of unspoiled Atlantic coastline, Fernandina Beach is a place where history meets coastal living. That coastal setting, however, brings a serious challenge for concrete surfaces: salt air corrosion. The constant exposure to ocean-driven salt spray accelerates concrete deterioration, causing spalling, pitting, and moisture intrusion that can compromise garage floors and commercial spaces. 904 Epoxy Floors provides licensed, insured, and professionally trained epoxy flooring installers who understand the unique demands of island living and deliver coatings built to resist Fernandina Beach's corrosive coastal environment.

Our Epoxy Flooring Process

  1. Free Consultation — We visit your Fernandina Beach property to measure and assess your concrete floor.
  2. Surface Preparation — Diamond grinding removes imperfections and opens the concrete pores for maximum adhesion.
  3. Crack & Joint Repair — We fill cracks and repair damaged areas for a smooth, level base.
  4. Epoxy Application — Multiple coats of commercial-grade epoxy are applied with your chosen color and flake pattern.
  5. Topcoat & Cure — A UV-stable polyaspartic or polyurethane topcoat seals everything for lasting durability.
