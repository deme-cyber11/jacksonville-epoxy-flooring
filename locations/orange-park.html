Epoxy Flooring in Orange Park, FL

Clay County's trusted epoxy floor coating specialists — serving Orange Park homeowners and businesses

Orange Park homeowners know that Florida's humidity is no friend to bare concrete. With Clay County averaging over 75% relative humidity year-round, unprotected garage floors absorb moisture that leads to staining, spalling, and the breakdown of the concrete surface. That's exactly why so many Orange Park residents are choosing professional epoxy flooring — a dense, non-porous coating that locks out moisture and transforms a utilitarian slab into a showroom-quality surface.

We've served Orange Park for years, coating garages in Oakleaf Plantation, Doctors Inlet, and the older established neighborhoods near Highway 17. What we've learned: Clay County soil has higher clay content, which means ground moisture migrates upward through concrete more aggressively than in sandy coastal areas. Our multi-layer moisture barrier system is specifically calibrated for this — we never skip the primer step, and we test concrete moisture levels before every install.

Orange Park's growing commercial corridor along Blanding Boulevard and Park Avenue also keeps our commercial epoxy crew busy. Auto dealerships, retail spaces, and light industrial tenants all benefit from the chemical resistance and ease of cleaning that a quality epoxy system delivers.

Our Installation Process

Every Orange Park project follows our proven 5-step process:

  1. Surface grinding — Diamond grinder opens the concrete pores for maximum adhesion
  2. Moisture barrier primer — Penetrating epoxy primer seals against Clay County's upward moisture migration
  3. Base coat — 100% solids epoxy base, rolled and back-rolled for even coverage
  4. Decorative topcoat — Flake broadcast, metallic pigment, or solid color per your choice
  5. 24-hour cure — Light foot traffic after 24 hours; vehicle traffic after 72 hours
