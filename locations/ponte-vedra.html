What Salt Air, Sun and Humidity Do to Ponte Vedra Floors

  • Salt air. Oceanfront and Intracoastal homes get a steady dose of salt on the sea breeze. Salt works into porous concrete and scales the surface, and it pits thin coatings.
  • UV exposure. Garages that stand open, patios and pool decks see some of the strongest sun in the state. Standard epoxy can yellow in UV light. A UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat keeps its color.
  • Moisture vapor. Ground moisture near the marshes and the Intracoastal Waterway pushes vapor up through slabs all year. That vapor is what lifts coatings applied without moisture testing and a vapor-blocking primer.
  • Hot tires and golf carts. Hot tires can lift basic single-coat epoxy, and daily golf cart traffic scuffs weak finishes. Flake systems and polyaspartic topcoats are built for both.

How We Handle It From First Call to Finished Floor

  1. Free estimate. We come to you anywhere in Ponte Vedra, look at the slab, test it for moisture and give you a written quote. The price is in writing before anything starts.
