Epoxy Flooring in Mandarin, FL

Professional garage and commercial floor coatings for Mandarin's established neighborhoods

Mandarin is one of Jacksonville's most beloved communities — mature oak tree canopies, riverfront properties along the St. Johns, strong neighborhood pride, and homes ranging from 1970s ranches to newer builds near Julington Creek. What these homes share is exposure to Jacksonville's signature humidity, which regularly hits 75–80% year-round in South Jacksonville. That persistent moisture is the enemy of bare concrete, slowly breaking down the surface, inviting stains, and making garage floors increasingly difficult to keep clean.

Mandarin homeowners tend to be practical and detail-oriented. They want a floor that solves real problems — motor oil that won't penetrate, rain water that wipes up instead of soaking in, and a surface that doesn't show every tire mark or garage spill. Our epoxy flooring systems deliver exactly that. The non-porous coating creates a moisture barrier that keeps Mandarin's humidity from attacking your concrete from below, while the hard surface resists staining from above.

We've coated garages all across Mandarin — from older ranch homes on Mandarin Road to the newer communities off Old St. Augustine Road and near Julington Creek Plantation. Older homes often have slabs with decades of oil and staining that require aggressive prep work; we handle this routinely and it's fully factored into your quote. Newer slabs need proper moisture testing — also standard in our process.

Our Installation Process

Every Mandarin project follows our proven 5-step installation:

  1. Surface grinding — Removes oil, paint, and contamination; profiles concrete for adhesion
  2. Moisture barrier primer — Penetrating primer stops vapor transmission through South Jacksonville's humid slabs
  3. Base coat — 100% solids epoxy laid thick for maximum durability
  4. Decorative topcoat — Your choice of flake blend, solid color, or metallic finish
  5. 24-hour cure — Walk on it the next day; vehicle traffic after 72 hours
