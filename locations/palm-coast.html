Palm Coast is Flagler County's largest city with a population of approximately 95,000, making it one of the fastest-growing communities in Northeast Florida. Originally developed by ITT Community Development Corporation in the 1970s as a master-planned community, Palm Coast saw its most explosive growth during the 2000s when thousands of single-family homes were built across neighborhoods like Belle Terre, Palm Harbor, Indian Trails, and the sections along Palm Coast Parkway. Today, those 15-to-25-year-old homes have garage floors showing their age: hairline cracks, surface wear, oil stains, and moisture discoloration are common across the city. Professional epoxy flooring is the most cost-effective way to restore and protect these slabs while transforming a tired garage into a clean, finished space. 904 Epoxy Floors sends licensed, insured, and professionally trained epoxy flooring installers to Palm Coast and all of Flagler County.

Our Epoxy Flooring Process

  1. Free Consultation — We visit your Palm Coast property to measure and assess your concrete floor.
  2. Surface Preparation — Diamond grinding removes imperfections and opens the concrete pores for maximum adhesion.
  3. Crack & Joint Repair — We fill cracks and repair damaged areas for a smooth, level base.
  4. Epoxy Application — Multiple coats of commercial-grade epoxy are applied with your chosen color and flake pattern.
  5. Topcoat & Cure — A UV-stable polyaspartic or polyurethane topcoat seals everything for lasting durability.

Frequently Asked Questions — Epoxy Flooring in Palm Coast

Why do so many Palm Coast homes need epoxy flooring?

Palm Coast was originally developed by ITT Community Development Corporation in the 1970s, and the majority of homes were built during the 2000s construction boom. These 15-to-25-year-old concrete garage floors are now reaching the age where surface wear, hairline cracking, and moisture staining become visible. Professional epoxy flooring restores and protects these slabs, adding years of life while transforming a dull gray garage into a clean, finished space. The Belle Terre and Palm Harbor areas have especially high demand due to the volume of homes from that era.

How long does an epoxy floor installation take in Palm Coast?

Most Palm Coast garage floors are completed in one to two days. Day one includes diamond grinding, crack repair, and primer application. Day two covers the epoxy base coat, decorative flake or metallic layer, and clear topcoat. You can walk on the floor after 24 hours and drive on it after 72 hours. Larger commercial projects may take an additional day depending on square footage.
