Epoxy Flooring in St. Augustine, FL

Durable, humidity-resistant epoxy coatings for St. Johns County homeowners and businesses

America's oldest city has seen a lot over 450 years, but the challenge of Florida's subtropical humidity never changes. St. Augustine sits at the intersection of coastal air from the Atlantic and inland moisture from the St. Johns River basin — a combination that makes bare concrete garage floors particularly vulnerable to moisture infiltration, staining, and surface decay. Epoxy flooring is the modern answer: a sealed, impermeable barrier that keeps moisture out while giving your floor a finish that would look right at home in any showroom.

We regularly serve St. Augustine's growing residential communities — Nocatee, World Golf Village, Palencia, and the historic neighborhoods closer to downtown. Each area has its own concrete challenge: Nocatee's newer builds have slabs that need a cure period before coating; older downtown properties often have slabs with decades of oil and contaminant buildup requiring more aggressive surface prep. We handle both.

St. Augustine's thriving tourist-driven commercial sector also benefits from our commercial epoxy systems. Restaurants, retail shops, and boutique hospitality businesses along the historic district count on floors that handle heavy foot traffic, Florida humidity, and frequent cleaning without showing wear. Our commercial-grade systems are built for exactly that.

Our Installation Process

Every St. Augustine installation follows our proven 5-step process:

  1. Surface grinding — Diamond-tipped grinders open concrete pores and remove old coatings
  2. Moisture barrier primer — Critical in St. Augustine's coastal zone; locks out ground moisture
  3. Base coat — High-solids epoxy base for structural integrity and adhesion
  4. Decorative topcoat — Flake, metallic pigment, or solid color per your specification
  5. 24-hour cure — Foot traffic safe after 24 hours; vehicles after 72 hours
