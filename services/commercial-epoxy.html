Why Choose Epoxy for Your Jacksonville Business?

  • Seamless surface: No grout lines or seams where moisture, bacteria, or contaminants can accumulate
  • Chemical resistance: Resists oils, acids, solvents, and cleaning chemicals that destroy bare concrete
  • Slip resistance: Anti-slip aggregates and broadcast media provide OSHA-compliant traction ratings
  • Brightness: High-gloss systems reflect light and can reduce lighting costs in warehouses
  • Fast installation: Most commercial projects are completed in 1–3 days with minimal business disruption
  • Long lifespan: Commercial epoxy systems last 15–25 years under normal use before major maintenance is needed

Commercial Epoxy Process

1
Site Assessment & Specification

We assess your facility, traffic loads, chemical exposure, and drainage requirements to specify the right system for your application.

2
Surface Preparation

Shot blasting or diamond grinding to the correct surface profile. Commercial prep is more aggressive than residential to ensure adhesion under heavy loads.

3
Crack & Joint Treatment

All cracks are repaired with structural epoxy. Control joints are treated with semi-rigid joint filler to prevent cracking at vulnerable points.

4
Multi-Layer System Application

Primer, body coat(s), and topcoat applied per the project specification. Commercial systems often use thicker builds (20–100 mils) versus residential (10–20 mils).

5
Return to Service

Most commercial systems allow foot traffic within 24 hours and vehicle/forklift traffic within 72 hours.

Commercial Epoxy FAQ

Can you work nights and weekends to avoid closing my business?

Yes — we regularly schedule commercial installations around business hours. Many Jacksonville restaurants and retail spaces have us work nights and weekends. Contact us to discuss scheduling that minimizes disruption to your operations.

Do you do line marking for warehouse floors?

Yes — safety line marking, bay markings, and directional arrows can be incorporated into the flooring system during installation. We use epoxy-based line marking paint for durability that outlasts paint applied to bare concrete by many years.
