Why It Matters

Florida Epoxy Is Different

Jacksonville's 75% average humidity creates specific failure modes. The #1 cause of peeling: moisture vapor transmission (MVT) through the concrete slab — and skipping the vapor-barrier primer step.

We test every slab, install the barrier, and build systems rated for Northeast Florida. That's why we offer a 15-year warranty — and mean it.
