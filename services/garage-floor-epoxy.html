What Is Garage Floor Epoxy?

Garage floor epoxy is a multi-layer polymer coating system applied directly to your concrete slab. Unlike paint (which sits on the surface and peels), epoxy chemically bonds to the concrete and creates an extremely hard, durable coating. The system typically consists of:

  • A moisture-barrier primer that bonds to the concrete and stops moisture vapor from pushing up through the slab
  • A base coat epoxy layer where decorative flakes or colors are broadcast
  • A UV-stable topcoat (polyurethane or polyaspartic) that provides scratch resistance, chemical resistance, and gloss

The result is a floor that looks like it belongs in a luxury car showroom, resists virtually everything you throw at it, and is easy to keep clean with a quick hose-down or mop.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does garage floor epoxy last in Jacksonville?

Our garage floor epoxy systems are warranted for 15 years against peeling, flaking, and delamination. With proper care, they typically last 15–20 years. Jacksonville's warm climate actually helps curing, though our moisture-barrier system is critical for longevity in Florida's humidity.

Can I install epoxy over my existing garage floor?

If your existing floor is bare concrete in reasonable condition, yes. We diamond grind the surface to create the right adhesion profile. If you have previous paint or coatings, they typically need to be removed first. We assess all of this during the free estimate.

How long do I have to stay out of the garage?

Light foot traffic is safe after 24 hours. You can drive into the garage after 72 hours. Full cure takes 7 days, but normal use can resume much sooner. We typically complete a 2-car garage in 1–2 days and you're back to using it within 3 days total.
