Garage Floor Epoxy Coating Jacksonville, FL

Multi-layer moisture-resistant systems engineered for Northeast Florida's subtropical climate

Jacksonville's Most Trusted Garage Floor Epoxy Installers

Your garage floor takes more abuse than any other surface in your home — cars dripping oil, lawn equipment leaking gas, bikes and tools scratching the surface, and Jacksonville's humidity working its way up through the slab from below. A bare concrete floor is porous, stainable, and gradually crumbling under all that punishment. A professionally installed garage floor epoxy transforms that same slab into a sealed, chemical-resistant, showroom-quality surface that stays beautiful for 15–20 years.

904 Epoxy Floors has coated 500+ garage floors across Jacksonville and Northeast Florida. What sets us apart from big-box DIY kits and budget installers? Our moisture-first approach. Jacksonville averages 75% relative humidity year-round, and that moisture vapor migrates upward through concrete 24 hours a day. DIY epoxy products and installers who skip proper prep fail for one reason: they trap that moisture between the concrete and the coating, causing delamination (peeling) within 1–3 years. We solve this with professional-grade diamond grinding and a vapor-blocking primer before any coating goes down.

Our Garage Floor Epoxy Installation Process

Every garage floor project follows our 5-step Florida-optimized installation:

1

Surface Grinding

We use professional diamond-tipped grinding equipment to open the concrete pores, remove surface contamination (oil, paint, old coatings), and create a proper surface profile. This step is non-negotiable — it's what separates a coating that lasts from one that fails.

2

Moisture Testing & Primer

We test concrete moisture vapor emission rate (MVER) before applying anything. For most Jacksonville garages, we apply a penetrating vapor-barrier primer that blocks the upward moisture transmission through the slab — the single most important step for longevity in Florida's climate.

3

Base Coat Application

Our 100% solids epoxy base coat is rolled and back-rolled to ensure even coverage and eliminate holidays (thin spots). This thick base creates the structural foundation of the system and provides the adhesion layer for the decorative topcoat.

4

Decorative Topcoat

Your choice of finish: full-broadcast colored flake (our most popular option), solid color, or metallic pigment. Anti-slip aggregate is broadcast into the topcoat while wet. The result is a seamless, decorative surface with built-in traction.

Cure time: Light foot traffic after 24 hours. Vehicle traffic after 72 hours. Full chemical cure in 7 days, though normal use can resume much sooner. Jacksonville's warm temperatures actually assist curing — we plan application timing around humidity conditions to ensure optimal results.

Garage Floor Epoxy Cost in Jacksonville

Garage floor epoxy in Jacksonville typically runs $3–$8 per square foot for professionally installed systems, with most 2-car garages (approximately 400–500 sq ft) falling in the $1,500–$3,500 range. Cost factors include:

  • Floor size — Larger garages have lower per-square-foot costs due to setup efficiency
  • Concrete condition — Slabs with significant oil staining, cracks, or pitting require more prep work
  • System type — Standard flake systems start around $3/sqft; metallic finishes run $5–8/sqft
  • Number of layers — Higher-moisture environments may require an additional primer coat
