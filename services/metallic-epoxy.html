Jacksonville Metallic Epoxy Flooring — One-of-a-Kind Custom Finishes

Custom 3D metallic designs with UV-stable pigments — no two floors are exactly alike · Free on-site consultations

Metallic epoxy is the most visually striking floor coating available — a system that combines an epoxy base with ultra-fine metallic and pearlescent pigments that flow, swirl, and create three-dimensional depth effects. The result looks like molten metal, rippling water, or polished stone, except it's on your floor and built to stay there for 20+ years. Every metallic installation is genuinely one-of-a-kind — the organic flowing patterns can't be exactly reproduced, which means your floor is as individual as your home.

When Standard Finishes Aren't Enough

Standard epoxy colors look functional. Metallic looks intentional. The difference matters when any of these apply:

  • You have a vehicle collection worth showing off — A pearl or champagne metallic floor makes every car in the garage look like it belongs in a showroom.
  • You want a designer finish, not a contractor finish — Flat solid-color coatings are forgettable. Metallic becomes a conversation piece in your own home.
  • Your previous epoxy yellowed in Florida's sun — If your garage faces south and gets hours of direct sunlight daily, standard epoxy topcoats chalk and yellow within a few years. Our UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat prevents this by design.
  • Interior floors where aesthetics matter — Showrooms, home gyms, restaurant and bar floors, office lobbies — metallic epoxy replaces expensive tile at a fraction of the installation time and cost.
  • You're preparing a home for sale — A metallic garage floor stops buyers in their tracks. Jacksonville's competitive real estate market rewards memorable finishes.

A gray concrete floor — or a flat, generic coating — is a missed opportunity. Done right, the floor becomes a feature people remember about your home.

How Metallic Epoxy Works

The final pattern is created live on your slab. The installer's technique is what makes each floor unique:

1

Diamond Grinding & Moisture Prep

Same meticulous surface preparation as any professional system. The concrete profile must be correct for metallic pigments to bond and flow properly. We apply the moisture vapor barrier primer on every Jacksonville metallic job — Florida's humidity doesn't discriminate based on coating type.

2

Solid Color Base Coat

A foundational epoxy layer establishes the base color — typically white, black, or a custom tone — that sets the depth of the final metallic effect. The base color affects how the metallic pigments appear overhead and at changing angles throughout the day.

3

Metallic Pigment Application

Ultra-fine mica particles coated in reflective materials are introduced into the wet epoxy and manipulated with squeegees, air tools, and hand techniques. The pigments move and create organic flowing patterns. Popular effects: ripple/wave, marble veining, lava flow, ocean/geode swirl.

4

UV-Stable Polyaspartic Topcoat

We always finish metallic floors with a 100% UV-stable aliphatic polyaspartic — never standard epoxy. In Florida, this is non-negotiable. The polyaspartic locks in the design, adds depth, and stays clear and non-yellowing for 20+ years regardless of sun exposure.

What Does Metallic Epoxy Cost in Jacksonville?

Metallic epoxy runs $7–$12 per square foot installed. A standard two-car garage (~500 sq ft) typically costs $3,500–$6,000, depending on design complexity, color count, and slab condition. Multi-color or detailed pattern work adds to installation time. All pricing is confirmed in writing before work starts — no surprises.

Compare that to high-end tile: metallic epoxy installs in a day or two and creates a seamless surface with no grout lines to clean. For the aesthetic impact relative to cost and timeline, the comparison isn't close.
