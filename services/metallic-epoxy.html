Metallic Epoxy Floors in Jacksonville, FL

Stunning 3D metallic finishes that transform garages and showrooms into works of art

Metallic epoxy flooring is in a category of its own. Unlike standard solid-color or flake epoxy systems, metallic epoxy uses highly reflective metallic pigments suspended in a clear epoxy resin to create flowing, three-dimensional patterns that look like liquid metal, swirling galaxies, or the deep ocean. No two metallic epoxy floors are exactly alike — each one is a custom creation based on your color choices and our application techniques.

904 Epoxy Floors has installed metallic epoxy throughout Jacksonville, Ponte Vedra, St. Augustine, and the surrounding Northeast Florida market. Our metallic systems are formulated with UV-stable pigments that won't yellow under Florida's intense coastal sun — a critical specification for homes near the Jacksonville beaches.

Our Metallic Epoxy Installation Process

1
Design Consultation

We review metallic color samples with you and discuss pattern preferences. The most popular combinations in Jacksonville include pearl, copper, silver galaxy, and ocean blue.

2
Surface Prep & Diamond Grinding

The concrete is diamond ground to the correct surface profile. Metallic epoxy requires a perfectly prepared surface — any contaminants or improper profiling will show in the final result.

3
Moisture Barrier Primer

Applied under climate-controlled conditions (45–55% humidity) to ensure adhesion in Jacksonville's humid environment.

4
Metallic Epoxy Application

Premium UV-stable metallic pigments are mixed into clear epoxy and applied. Our team uses specialized techniques — squeegees, rollers, and propane torches — to manipulate the metallic finish into the desired patterns as the epoxy flows and sets.

5
Polyaspartic Topcoat

A high-gloss polyaspartic topcoat is applied over the metallic layer to protect the design and provide long-term durability and UV resistance.

FAQ — Metallic Epoxy

How do I choose metallic epoxy colors?

We bring physical color samples to your home so you can see them in your actual lighting. Popular choices in Jacksonville include pearl/silver (pairs well with luxury vehicles), ocean blue/turquoise (very popular at the beaches), and copper/bronze (works beautifully in homes with warm wood tones).

Will metallic epoxy yellow in Florida's sun?

Cheap metallic epoxy pigments do yellow in UV-intensive environments. We exclusively use UV-stable metallic pigments and UV-stable polyaspartic topcoats that are rated for exterior and near-window applications. Our installations at Jacksonville Beach and Ponte Vedra waterfront homes have maintained their color for years.

Is metallic epoxy as durable as standard flake epoxy?

Yes — metallic epoxy is equally durable for everyday garage use. It handles vehicle traffic, oil drips, and foot traffic the same as our flake systems. The only difference is that metallic floors show fine scratches more easily than flake systems, so we recommend avoiding dragging heavy metal objects across the surface.
