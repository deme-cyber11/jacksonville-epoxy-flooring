Polyaspartic Floor Coatings in Jacksonville, FL

The premium alternative to standard epoxy — UV-stable, faster cure, and built for Florida's sun and humidity

Polyaspartic coatings are a newer generation of aliphatic polyurea floor coating that outperform standard epoxy in several important ways — particularly for Jacksonville's specific climate challenges. Where epoxy can yellow and degrade under intense UV exposure, polyaspartic is fully UV-stable. Where epoxy requires exact temperature and humidity conditions during application, polyaspartic has a broader application window. And where standard epoxy takes 5–7 days to fully cure, polyaspartic floors are ready for vehicle traffic in as little as 24 hours.

At 904 Epoxy Floors, we use polyaspartic as the topcoat layer on all of our residential and commercial systems — it provides the UV resistance, scratch resistance, and clarity that give our floors their long-term performance and warranty. We also offer full 100% polyaspartic floor coating systems (primer to topcoat all polyaspartic) for customers who want the fastest possible cure times or have applications where UV resistance is especially critical.

Polyaspartic vs. Epoxy: What's the Difference?

Feature Standard Epoxy Polyaspartic
UV Stability ❌ Yellows over time ✅ Fully UV-stable
Cure Time 5–7 days full cure 24–48 hour full cure
Temperature Range 50–90°F optimal -30°F to 140°F
Abrasion Resistance Good Excellent

Best For Interior base coats All applications, especially near-window and coastal

Benefits of Polyaspartic Coating in Jacksonville

  • UV resistance: Won't yellow or amber under Jacksonville's intense Florida sun — critical for garages with windows, near-door areas, and outdoor applications
  • Fast return to service: 24-hour vehicle traffic is standard — ideal for commercial facilities or homeowners who can't be out of their garage for a week
  • Superior scratch resistance: Harder than standard epoxy topcoats — handles everyday floor contact better over time
  • Temperature flexibility: Can be applied during Jacksonville's summer heat when standard epoxy has a narrower cure window
  • Salt resistance: Excellent for coastal areas like Fernandina Beach, Jacksonville Beach, and Ponte Vedra
  • Crystal clarity: Maintains a clearer, higher-gloss appearance longer than standard epoxy topcoats

Polyaspartic Installation Process

1
Surface Prep & Diamond Grinding

The concrete slab is prepared to the correct surface profile for chemical adhesion of the polyaspartic system.

2
Primer Coat

A penetrating polyaspartic or epoxy primer bonds to the concrete and seals moisture vapor.

3
Decorative Layer

Vinyl flake, metallic pigments, or solid color is broadcast into the base coat for the desired appearance.

4
Polyaspartic Topcoat

The UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat is applied at the specified wet film thickness for maximum performance.

5
Fast Cure

Foot traffic in 24 hours. Vehicle traffic in 24–48 hours. Full service in 48 hours for most applications.

Polyaspartic FAQ

Is polyaspartic worth the extra cost over standard epoxy?

For Jacksonville properties, usually yes. The UV stability alone makes it worth the premium for any garage with windows or near-door areas. The faster cure time also reduces inconvenience significantly. We use polyaspartic as the topcoat on all of our systems for these reasons.

How much does polyaspartic flooring cost in Jacksonville?

A full polyaspartic system (all layers) costs $5–$9 per square foot. Since we include polyaspartic topcoats in all of our standard systems, you're already getting polyaspartic UV resistance in most of our pricing. A full polyaspartic-only system is priced slightly higher than our hybrid epoxy/polyaspartic system.

Is polyaspartic good for outdoor Jacksonville applications?

Yes — polyaspartic is one of the only floor coating systems suitable for direct outdoor use. It's used on covered patios, pool decks, and exterior walkways where UV exposure would degrade standard epoxy within 1–2 years. If you have an outdoor concrete application, polyaspartic or a polyurea system is the right choice.
