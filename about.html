Why We Started

Built for Florida's Climate. Not a Generic Coating.

Most epoxy failures in Jacksonville come down to one thing: installers who don't account for Florida's humidity. Jacksonville slabs transmit moisture vapor constantly — and when you apply epoxy without testing for it, the coating delaminates within 12–18 months.

We built this business around getting that step right. Every job starts with moisture vapor transmission testing. Every slab gets diamond-ground, not acid-etched. That's why our 15-year warranty isn't a marketing gimmick — it's backed by a process we know works in Florida.

  • MVT testing on every slab — no exceptions
  • Diamond grinding, not acid etching
  • 100% solids epoxy — not water-based
  • Florida-licensed contractor — fully insured

What Sets Our Process Apart

This is what we do differently from most Jacksonville epoxy installers — and why it matters for how long your floor lasts.

Moisture Vapor Testing First

We test every slab for moisture vapor transmission before applying a single coat. If a slab fails, we apply a penetrating vapor-barrier primer. Most installers skip this — it's the #1 reason epoxy peels in Florida.

Diamond Grinding, Not Acid Etching

Acid etching opens pores but leaves a weak surface layer. Diamond grinding removes that layer entirely and creates a mechanical profile that epoxy bonds to permanently. It costs more but it's why our floors last.

15-Year Written Warranty

We put our warranty in writing because our process backs it up. Delamination, peeling, or coating failure within 15 years? We come back and fix it — no charge. That's not a gimmick; it's confidence in our work.
