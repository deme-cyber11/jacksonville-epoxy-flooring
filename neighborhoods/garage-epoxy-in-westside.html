Our Garage Floor Epoxy Process in Westside

When you choose Jacksonville Epoxy Flooring Pros for your Westside garage floor epoxy project, you can expect a comprehensive, professional experience from start to finish.

Initial Consultation and Assessment

We begin with a free, no-obligation consultation at your Westside property. Our experts assess the existing floor condition, take precise measurements, and discuss your goals and preferences. We'll examine moisture levels, existing coatings, and any repairs needed before installation.

Surface Preparation

Proper preparation is the foundation of a lasting garage floor epoxy installation. Our team uses professional diamond grinding equipment to create the ideal surface profile for coating adhesion. We repair cracks, fill joints, and ensure the substrate is clean and ready for application.

Professional Application

Florida's mild winters provide ideal conditions for epoxy installation with faster cure times. Our experienced installers apply your chosen garage floor epoxy system using proven techniques that ensure uniform coverage, proper thickness, and optimal curing. We maintain strict quality control throughout the process.
