Whether you're considering epoxy for your garage, a commercial warehouse, or a showroom, the first question is always the same: what will it actually cost? The answer depends on the type of system, the size and condition of your floor, and the level of decorative finish you want. This guide covers real Jacksonville-area pricing based on our experience completing 500+ projects throughout Northeast Florida.

Factors That Affect Epoxy Flooring Cost

1. Floor Size

Larger projects generally cost less per square foot due to economies of scale. A 400 sq ft 2-car garage might cost $4–$7/sq ft installed, while a 200 sq ft 1-car garage might run $5–$8/sq ft for the same system since mobilization and setup costs are spread over fewer square feet.

2. Concrete Condition

The condition of your existing concrete slab is the biggest variable after system type. A clean, newer slab in good condition costs less to prepare than a 30-year-old slab with cracks, oil stains, or previous coatings that need removal. Expect $0–$2/sq ft in additional prep costs for challenging conditions.

3. System Type & Decorative Finish

A basic solid-color epoxy with a clear topcoat is the most affordable option. Full-broadcast flake systems add cost for the decorative media and the additional topcoat needed to encapsulate the flakes. Metallic epoxy is the premium option — the materials cost more and the application requires significantly more skill and time to achieve the flowing 3D patterns.

4. Moisture Barriers

In Jacksonville's humid subtropical climate, a moisture-barrier primer is non-negotiable for long-term performance. Some budget quotes exclude it to appear cheaper — but without it, the epoxy will eventually bubble, peel, or delaminate as moisture vapor pushes up through the Florida slab. Our quotes always include moisture testing and barrier primers.

5. Topcoat Choice

A standard epoxy topcoat is adequate for interior garages. A polyaspartic topcoat (which we use on all of our installations) adds UV resistance, superior scratch resistance, and faster cure time. For outdoor or near-window applications, polyaspartic is the right choice — and it's included in our standard pricing.

DIY vs. Professional Epoxy: Is It Worth Doing Yourself?

Big-box store epoxy kits cost $50–$300 and are tempting for budget-conscious homeowners. Here's why we strongly recommend professional installation in Jacksonville:

  • Adhesion failure: DIY kits don't include moisture-barrier primers. In Florida's humidity, this means the coating often peels within 1–3 years. Professional systems with moisture barriers last 15–20 years.
  • Surface prep matters: Without industrial diamond grinding, the concrete isn't properly profiled and the epoxy can't achieve permanent adhesion. No consumer tool replicates professional diamond grinding.
  • UV yellowing: Most DIY kits use amine-cured epoxy that yellows in UV light. Professional polyaspartic topcoats are UV-stable.
  • Appearance: Professional flake broadcast and metallic application techniques produce dramatically better results than any DIY method.
  • Warranty: DIY kits carry no meaningful warranty. Our installations carry a 15-year warranty against peeling, flaking, and delamination.
💰 Bottom line: A DIY kit that costs $200 and lasts 2 years costs $100/year. A professional installation at $2,500 that lasts 15 years costs $167/year — and looks dramatically better the entire time. The "savings" of DIY are usually an illusion once you factor in lifespan and appearance.

How to Get the Best Price on Epoxy Flooring

  1. Get 2–3 quotes: Compare itemized quotes from licensed contractors. Be specific about what's included — primer, topcoat type, number of coats, and warranty terms.
  2. Ask about moisture testing: Any reputable Jacksonville contractor will test for moisture vapor transmission before quoting. If they don't test, they don't understand Florida concrete.
  3. Check the warranty: 15-year warranties are the industry standard for quality residential work. If you're offered less, the materials or installation methods may be inferior.
  4. Verify licensing: Florida requires contractors to be licensed. Verify before signing any agreement.
  5. Bundle if possible: Doing multiple garages (yours and a neighbor's) or combining with other concrete work can sometimes reduce per-project pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does epoxy flooring add home value in Jacksonville?

Yes — a professionally installed epoxy garage floor is a recognized selling feature in the Jacksonville real estate market. Most buyers view it as a desirable upgrade that signals a well-maintained home. The $1,500–$3,500 cost is generally considered a strong pre-sale investment, especially in neighborhoods like Ponte Vedra, Mandarin, and Fleming Island.

Is epoxy flooring cheaper than tile for a garage?

Yes — significantly. Tile flooring in a garage costs $8–$15 per square foot installed and creates grout lines where moisture and stains collect. Epoxy at $3–$7/sq ft is more affordable, more durable for garage use, and creates a seamless, easy-to-clean surface. For garage applications, epoxy is the superior choice in almost every case.

How often does epoxy flooring need to be redone?

A professionally installed epoxy floor with moisture barriers and polyaspartic topcoat lasts 15–20 years in Jacksonville with normal residential use. Commercial floors under heavier traffic last 10–15 years. When the topcoat eventually wears, it can often be re-topcoated at a fraction of the original cost — typically $1–$3/sq ft — without redoing the entire system.
