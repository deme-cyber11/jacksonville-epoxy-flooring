The Florida Problem

Epoxy That Peels Isn't an Epoxy Problem — It's a Prep Problem

If you've seen epoxy floors peel in Florida — bubbling up in patches, lifting at the edges, separating from the concrete after one summer — you've seen what happens when the moisture issue isn't addressed before the coating goes down.

Jacksonville averages 75% humidity year-round. Concrete slabs transmit moisture vapor from the ground up constantly. A coating applied without proper vapor barrier prep will fail. Sometimes in months, sometimes in two years — but it will fail.

How It Works

Our 5-Step Florida-Grade Process

What separates a floor that lasts 15 years from one that peels in 18 months is entirely in the preparation.

1

Free On-Site Assessment

Inspect concrete, test moisture vapor (MVT), and provide a written fixed-price quote. MVT testing is non-negotiable in Florida.

2

Diamond Grinding & Repair

Diamond grinding (not acid etching) profiles the surface for maximum mechanical bonding. Cracks and spalls filled.

3

Moisture-Vapor Barrier

Penetrating vapor-barrier primer applied before any topcoat. The step most peeling jobs skipped.

4

Coating System

Base coat, decorative layer, and UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat. Applied with temperature monitoring.
